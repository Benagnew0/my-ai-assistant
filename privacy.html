Privacy policy

My AI Assistant · last updated October 9, 2026

My AI Assistant ("the app") is a personal Android app made by Ben Agnew for his own use and a small number of friends. This policy explains what the app does with your data, including data it receives from Google APIs.

Everything stays on your phone

The app has no server. Your tasks, notes, documents, reminders, calendar view, contacts lookups and email are stored only on your phone, in an encrypted database. The app's AI runs on the phone; your data is never sent to an AI service. The developer never receives your data.

Google user data

If you connect a Google account, the app asks for these permissions:

  • Read your Gmail (gmail.readonly): to show your recent email in the app, mark which messages need a reply, summarise them on the phone, and let you turn an email into a task.
  • Send email as you (gmail.send): only when you tap Send on a message you have reviewed. The app never sends email on its own.
  • Files the app creates in your Google Drive (drive.file): only if you turn on backup, to store and restore your own encrypted backups. The app cannot see any other Drive files.

Email is downloaded directly from Google to your phone and kept there, encrypted, for a limited time (30 days by default). It is not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI models. Backups uploaded to your Drive are encrypted on the phone with a passphrase only you know.

My AI Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other permissions

Calendar, contacts, microphone (speech is converted to text on the phone), camera and notifications are used only for the features that need them, only on the phone, and only after you allow them.

No tracking

The app has no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services. Apart from Google's APIs, it only contacts the internet to download its AI models and to check for app updates.

Removing your data

Disconnecting a Google account in the app's settings removes its access and deletes its cached email from the phone. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone.
