My AI Assistant

A personal Android assistant for tasks, notes, reminders, calendar and Gmail, with its AI running entirely on the phone.

My AI Assistant is a private app built by Ben Agnew for his own use and a few friends. It is not on the Play Store and is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Keeps tasks, checklists, notes, documents, reminders, renewals and habits in an encrypted database on the phone.
  • Shows the phone's calendar next to tasks.
  • Optionally connects Gmail accounts to show one inbox, mark which messages need a reply, turn emails into tasks and send replies the user has reviewed.
  • Runs its AI models on the phone. Nothing is sent to an AI service.
